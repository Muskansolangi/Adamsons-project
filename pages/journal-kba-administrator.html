Advocate Muhammad Ajmal Solangi Appointed Administrator, Karachi Bar Association

Advocate Muhammad Ajmal Solangi, Founder and Senior Partner of Adamsons Law Associates Karachi, was appointed Administrator of the Karachi Bar Association (KBA) on 15 December 2025. In this significant role, he was entrusted with planning, coordinating, and supervising the Association's elections through its Electronic Voting Machine (EVM) System.

A Role of Critical Importance

The Karachi Bar Association is one of the largest and most influential bar associations in Pakistan. The appointment of its Administrator is a position of immense responsibility, requiring integrity, organizational capability, and a deep understanding of the legal community's needs. Advocate Muhammad Ajmal Solangi's appointment reflects the trust and confidence that the legal fraternity places in his leadership and administrative abilities.

Electronic Voting Machine (EVM) System Supervision

A key responsibility of this role was overseeing the implementation and supervision of the Association's elections through its Electronic Voting Machine (EVM) System. This modern approach to conducting elections requires meticulous planning, technical oversight, and the ability to ensure a transparent and fair process. Advocate Muhammad Ajmal Solangi brought his extensive experience and legal expertise to ensure the smooth operation of this critical electoral process.

Building on a Legacy of Leadership

This appointment as Administrator of the Karachi Bar Association is the latest in a series of leadership roles that Advocate Muhammad Ajmal Solangi has undertaken in service of Pakistan's legal community. It follows his appointment as Additional Election Commissioner for the Sindh High Court Bar Association and further cements his reputation as a trusted leader within the legal profession.
