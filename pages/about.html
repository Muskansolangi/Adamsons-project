Who We Are

About Adamsons Law Associates

A full-service law firm built on accuracy of advice, client-first service, and integrity.

Our Firm

Leading Legal Practice Across Sindh

Adamsons Law Associates is a full-service law firm based in Karachi, with offices extending across Sindh. Since 2006, the Firm has built a practice defined by constitutional and civil litigation, corporate advisory, and dispute resolution — serving corporations, financial institutions, and private clients alike.

Our approach pairs rigorous legal research with a genuine understanding of each client's commercial and personal stakes, delivering counsel that is both legally sound and practically achievable.

What Guides Us

Philosophy, Vision & Mission

Our Philosophy

We believe in accuracy of advice. Every client's interests are paramount, and each is regularly consulted and updated at every stage of the legal process.

Our Vision

To place client satisfaction at the centre of everything we do, by rendering prompt, timely, and effective legal services at every stage of representation.

Our Mission

To build a law firm focused on quality legal service, dedicated to client care and noted for its integrity.

Founder & Senior Partner

Advocate Muhammad Ajmal Solangi

Chief Executive & Senior Partner

21+Years in Practice
15+Years Before the High Court
2006Established & Registered
2006LL.B, University of Karachi

Advocate Muhammad Ajmal Solangi is a distinguished legal practitioner with more than twenty one years of professional experience, including over fifteen years before the High Court of Sindh, and appears before the Supreme Court of Pakistan. He has built an outstanding reputation across constitutional, civil, criminal, banking, taxation, labour, corporate, and regulatory litigation.

  • —Represented multinational companies, financial institutions, banks, and government departments before superior and subordinate courts.
  • —Distinguished practice in strategic litigation, contract drafting, arbitration, and regulatory compliance.
  • —Additional Election Commissioner, Sindh High Court Bar Association.
  • —Administrator, Karachi Bar Association.
  • —General Secretary, Sindh Solangi Welfare Organization.

Education: LL.B., University of Karachi, Sindh Muslim Law College (2006) — dissertation on the Protection of Trade Marks and the Law of Passing Off; B.Com, University of Karachi, Government Commerce & Economics College (2002).
