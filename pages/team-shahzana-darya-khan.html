Advocate Shahzana Darya Khan — Family & Civil Law Expert in Karachi

03133438069 s.shehzuofficial@gmail.com
Family LawCivil LawCriminal LawProperty LawSharia Law

Advocate Shahzana Darya Khan is a licensed Advocate with the Sindh Bar Council, practising in Karachi since January 2023. She specializes in family law (divorce, khula, child custody, maintenance, inheritance), civil law, criminal law, and property law.

She regularly appears before courts in Karachi and Naushahro Feroze and has advised more than fifty family court clients. Her practice includes drafting plaints, written statements, bail applications, and affidavits. She also provides remote consultations for overseas Pakistani clients on matters of attestations and Power of Attorney.
