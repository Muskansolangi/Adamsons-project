Legal Practice Areas in Karachi

01

Corporate & Commercial Law

Advisory and representation on corporate structuring, company formation, commercial contracts, shareholder matters, and regulatory compliance before bodies such as the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan.

02

Constitutional & Civil Litigation

Representation before the Sindh High Court and subordinate courts in constitutional petitions, writ proceedings, and civil disputes.

03

Banking & Financial Laws

Counsel on banking recovery litigation, financial regulation, and disputes before the Banking Courts and Banking Tribunals.

04

Criminal Law

Defence and representation in criminal proceedings at all judicial forums, from Magistrate Courts to the High Court.

05

Taxation & Labour Law

Advisory on tax compliance and disputes, together with representation in labour and employment matters before Labour Courts and relevant tribunals.

06

Arbitration & Alternative Dispute Resolution

Representation of corporate entities, commercial organisations, and private institutions in arbitration proceedings involving commercial contracts and infrastructure projects.

Intellectual Property

Protection and enforcement of intellectual property rights, including advisory on registration, infringement, and related commercial matters.
