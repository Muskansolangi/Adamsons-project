Advocate Muhammad Ajmal Solangi

21+Years in Practice
15+Years Before the High Court
2006Established & Registered
2006LL.B, University of Karachi

Advocate Muhammad Ajmal Solangi is a distinguished legal practitioner with more than twenty one years of professional experience, including over fifteen years before the High Court of Sindh, and appears before the Supreme Court of Pakistan. He has built an outstanding reputation across constitutional, civil, criminal, banking, taxation, labour, corporate, and regulatory litigation.

  • —Represented multinational companies, financial institutions, banks, and government departments before superior and subordinate courts.
  • —Distinguished practice in strategic litigation, contract drafting, arbitration, and regulatory compliance.
  • —Additional Election Commissioner, Sindh High Court Bar Association.
  • —Administrator, Karachi Bar Association.
  • —General Secretary, Sindh Solangi Welfare Organization.

Education
LL.B., University of Karachi, Sindh Muslim Law College (2006) — dissertation on the Protection of Trade Marks and the Law of Passing Off; B.Com, University of Karachi, Government Commerce & Economics College (2002).

    Professional Distinctions

    Professional distinctions and public service within Pakistan's legal fraternity.

    2024 – 2026

    Additional Election Commissioner, Sindh High Court Bar Association

    Advocate Muhammad Ajmal Solangi was appointed Additional Election Commissioner for the elections of the Sindh High Court Bar Association, Karachi, for three consecutive terms. He played a pivotal role in ensuring the electoral process was conducted with complete transparency, fairness, and impartiality.

    December 2025

    Administrator, Karachi Bar Association — Electronic Voting System

    Following his appointment as Administrator of the Karachi Bar Association on 15 December 2025, Advocate Muhammad Ajmal Solangi was entrusted with planning, coordinating, and supervising the Association's elections through its Electronic Voting Machine (EVM) System.

    Recognition

    Lifetime Membership, Sindh High Court Bar Association

    In recognition of sustained professional competence and integrity, Advocate Muhammad Ajmal Solangi has received Certificates of Appreciation, Performance Awards, and Lifetime Membership from the Sindh High Court Bar Association.

    Public Service

    Civic Engagement

    Advocate Muhammad Ajmal Solangi contested the General Elections 2018 as a candidate for the Provincial Assembly of Sindh, reflecting a broader commitment to public service alongside his legal career.
