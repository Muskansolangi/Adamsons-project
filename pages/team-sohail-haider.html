Advocate Sohail Hyder — High Court Advocate with 7+ Years Experience

Advocate Sohail Hyder is a distinguished Advocate of the High Court with a law degree from Shah Abdul Latif University, Khairpur, and over seven years of legal experience. He represents clients before the High Court of Sindh and other relevant courts across Karachi and Sindh.

His practice encompasses Family Law (divorce, khula, child custody, maintenance), Civil Law, Criminal Law, Property Law, and Sharia Law. Advocate Sohail Hyder is committed to providing professional legal representation and practical guidance to clients across a wide range of legal matters, with a focus on effective and diligent handling of each case.
